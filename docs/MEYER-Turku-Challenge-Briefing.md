## The Challenge

> **How can ship designs be checked against rules and regulations automatically?**

### Why regulation and compliance checking?

1. **Complex regulatory landscape:** many overlapping class rules, conventions and regulations.
2. **Manual and time consuming:** compliance checking requires engineering expertise and substantial effort.
3. **Earlier detection, less downstream impact:** identify compliance issues before they propagate through the design.

---

## Task Description

**Develop and demonstrate an AI solution that checks ship design against rules and regulations.**

The rules and regulations can be, for example:

- **SOLAS:** International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea
- **MARPOL:** International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships
- **COLREG:** International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea
- **Classification Society Rules**, e.g. DNV or Lloyds
- **PVAG:** Passenger Vessel Accessibility Guidelines
- **ADA:** Americans with Disabilities Act

> We are looking for a solution that assists our engineers in their day-to-day work and informs them of any issues found in the design.

## Evaluation: What Your Demo Should Include

| Accuracy | Design Interpretation | Quality & Usability |
|---|---|---|
| Correctly identifies compliant vs. non-compliant design | Demonstrates that it can understand documents, drawings and/or 3D models | Solid technical foundation |
| Minimizes incorrect or misleading compliance findings | Clearly highlights why something is not compliant | Usability in an engineering context |
| *Can we trust the assessment?* | *Can it understand the design and show us why there is a problem?* | Clear demonstration of how the solution works |
| | | *Is this a credible foundation for a useful engineering tool?* |

> **A robust foundation matters more than a polished UI.**

## Appendix: Rules and Regulations

The "Availability" column is as stated in the briefing. It is not explained there, but presumably indicates how fully the rule text is available to participants.

| Rule / regulation | Availability | What does it cover? |
|---|---|---|
| **SOLAS** | Partially | International convention covering the safety of ships, including requirements intended to protect life at sea. |
| **MARPOL** | Partially | International convention focused on preventing pollution from ships. |
| **COLREG** | Yes | International rules governing ship navigation to prevent collisions at sea. |
| **Load Line Convention** | Partially | International convention establishing load-line requirements for ships. |
| **Ballast Water Management Convention** | Partially | Establishes requirements for the management of ships' ballast water and sediments. |
| **Classification Society Rules**, e.g. DNV | Yes | Technical classification rules and standards against which ship designs can be assessed. |
| **PVAG** (Passenger Vessel Accessibility Guidelines) | Yes | U.S. guidelines defining accessibility requirements for passenger vessels. |
