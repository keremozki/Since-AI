So on 2026-11-06 the July 2026 edition is published but not yet in force; the edition in force today is presumably the previous main edition (July 2025), but this could not be confirmed from a DNV document. Differences were not analysed. **Which edition should the project use: the one in force today, or the July 2026 edition (in force 1 January 2027)?**

## 4. COLREG
1. **Not the IMO edition.** The text is the USCG handbook (International pages only) + the USCG Amalgamated page for Part F. Differences from the IMO text could not be checked because the IMO edition was not retrieved.
2. Differences visible in the USCG publications themselves (quoted as printed):
   - The handbook contents page: "Footnotes proceeded by the double-dagger (‡) symbol indicates clarifications added by the U.S. Coast Guard Office of Navigation Systems; and, are not found in the original source material." For other examples, see https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules- amalgamated#specialrules."
- PDF page 117: "‡ Per 33 CFR 81.20, each vessel under the 72 COLREGS, except the vessels of the Navy, is exempt from the requirements of the 72 COLREGS to the limitation for the period of time stated in Rule 38 (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f), and (g) if: (a) Her keel was laid or is at a corresponding stage of construction before July 15, 1977; and, (b) She meets the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1960 (77 Stat. 194, 33 U.S.C. 1051-1094)."
- PDF page 133: "* Refer to the International Code of Safety for High-Speed Craft, 1994 and the International Code of Safety for High-Speed Craft, 2000."
- PDF page 143: "‡ The U.S. Coast Guard terminated monitoring DSC safety and distress frequency 2187.5 kHz on August 1, 2013. (78 FR 42102)."
   - The ‡ marks themselves remain inside the printed text of Rule 1(c) ("... under these Rules ‡.") and Annex IV 1(l) ("2187.5 kHz ‡"), and the Rule 38 title ("Exemptions ‡"); they are kept in the quotes as printed.
   - The handbook does **not print Part F (Rules 39-41, Verification of compliance)**; its table of contents ends at Rule 38. The Amalgamated page includes Part F; Rules 39-41 were taken from there.
   - The Amalgamated page inserts USCG annotations in curly brackets into the International text, e.g. Rule 39(b)/(c) "the IMO {International Maritime Organization} ...". They are kept in the quotes as printed and flagged in notes.
   - The handbook uses U.S. spelling throughout (e.g. "meters", "maneuver", "draft", "color"). Whether the IMO text spells these differently could not be checked.
   - The handbook (corrected 8 August 2024) and the Amalgamated page ("as amended June 12th, 2023", changed 08/21/2024) are two USCG renderings with different conventions (e.g. Rule 38(a) handbook "until four years", Amalgamated "until 4 years"). They were not diffed paragraph by paragraph (only Part F is taken from the Amalgamated page).
3. PDF text-layer limits: formulas and exponents are flattened (Annex I 8(a) "I = 3.43 x 106 x T x D2 x K-D", Annex I 13(b) formula, Annex III table "2x10-5 N/m2"). These rules are verified: false; read the numbers from the PDF page given in source_url.
4. Rule unit: one lettered paragraph = one rule; roman sub-items (i), (ii) stay inside their paragraph (not split). Rule 38(d) continues across a page as "(d) (continued)"; it is stored as COLREG-R38d and COLREG-R38d-part2 (two text pieces of one paragraph).
5. **DECISION – ship types.** 87 COLREG paragraphs name a COLREG vessel category (power-driven vessel, sailing vessel, vessel engaged in fishing, towing, high-speed craft, ...) and are filed under "unassigned". With the mapping table in README.md (team assumption) most "power-driven vessel" rules would apply to all three types. Please confirm the mapping.

## 5. Method decisions taken (please confirm or change)
1. PVAG/ADA: one rule = one numbered provision with text; each defined term = one entry; advisories and figures excluded.
2. Structured fields (parameter/operator/threshold/unit) are derived by pattern from the quote. Only single-limit clauses and the manually reviewed ones are verified: true; 274 rules are verified: false (list in README.md) and need a manual structuring pass before the rule engine uses their numbers.
3. "PVAG Chapters V1-V10 → all-ship-types" (both Large vessels and Ferries get the same chapters). Chapter V11 (tenders) → unassigned.
4. Out of scope and not collected: SOLAS, FSS Code, HSC Code, MARPOL, Load Line, Ballast Water.
